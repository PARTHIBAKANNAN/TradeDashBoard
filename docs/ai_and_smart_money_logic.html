🧠 Same-Slot Benchmarking Philosophy
20-Day Archive

Standard volume indicators fail because midday volume is naturally lower than open rush. Smart Money Engine compares each 5-minute candle strictly against that exact same time slot across the last 20 trading days.

Metric Formula Purpose
Fresh Turnover $\text{Close} \times \text{Volume}$ Rupee-value velocity in current 5-min bucket.
Fresh Turnover Ratio $\frac{\text{Today Turnover}}{\text{Avg Slot Turnover (20d)}}$ Institutional accumulation vs historical baseline.
RVOL $\frac{\text{Today Volume}}{\text{Avg Slot Volume (20d)}}$ Share volume expansion ratio.
Smart Money RS $\% \Delta \text{Stock}_{\text{open}} - \% \Delta \text{NIFTY}_{\text{open}}$ Intraday momentum anchored to today's open price.

📐 Dynamic Stop Loss & Target Math
ATR + Swing Anchored

Stop Loss is never fixed. It dynamically expands/contracts based on true 5-minute volatility ($1.5 \times ATR_{14}$) and 5-candle swing lows/highs with hard boundaries:

$$SL\_Dist = \min\Big(\max(\text{Swing Dist},\, 1.5 \cdot ATR_{14},\, 0.0085 \cdot LTP),\, 0.0200 \cdot LTP\Big)$$ $$\text{Target} = Entry \pm (2.0 \times SL\_Dist) \quad \text{[1:2 Risk-Reward]}$$
Trailing Stop Loss (TSL) $\ge 1.20\%$

$\text{TSL Offset} = \text{Peak Price} \times \max(SL\_Pct, 1.2\%)$. Only ever ratchets favorably.

Auto-Breakeven Guarantee +1.0R Reached

When $\text{Peak} \ge Entry + R$, Stop Loss ratchets to $Entry + \text{buffer}$ eliminating downside risk.
